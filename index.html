Privacy Policy for HomeSlate

Effective Date: November 22, 2026

HomeSlate is an offline-first educational quiz and handwriting practice app designed for families. Your family's privacy is built into the architecture of the app.

1. Zero Data Collected by Us

HomeSlate does not operate any backend servers and never collects, views, stores, or transmits your family's profiles, child names, quizzes, answers, or handwriting strokes to us or any third party. No user accounts, email addresses, or phone numbers are required to use the app.

2. Local Device Storage & System Backups

All app data—including profile names, optional PIN hashes, quizzes, grades, and compressed handwriting files—is stored locally on your device. Depending on your device settings, this local app data may be included in your personal encrypted Apple iCloud Backup or Google Android Backup, or in manual backup files you choose to export to your own cloud storage.

3. Local Home Wi-Fi Sync (Optional)

When connecting parent and student devices on the same home Wi-Fi network, quizzes and answers transfer directly between your devices over your local router without passing through the internet or external servers.

4. Children's Privacy

HomeSlate complies with children's privacy standards (including COPPA, GDPR-K, and India's DPDP Act) by design. The app contains zero advertisements, zero third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, and requires a Parental Gate challenge before opening external links or making store purchases.

5. Data Control & Deletion

Parents have full control over all data stored in the app. You can edit or delete any child profile, quiz, or submission directly inside the app at any time, or remove all local data by uninstalling the app.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@kalimaath.net
