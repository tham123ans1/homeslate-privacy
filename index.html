Privacy Policy for HomeSlate

Effective Date: November 22, 2026

HomeSlate is an offline-first educational quiz and handwriting practice app designed for families. Your family's privacy is built into the architecture of the app.

1. Zero Data Collected by Us

HomeSlate does not operate any backend servers and never collects, views, stores, or transmits your family's profiles, child names, quizzes, answers, or handwriting strokes to us or any third party. No user accounts, email addresses, or phone numbers are required to use the app.

2. Local Device Storage & System Backups

All app data—including profile names, optional PIN hashes, quizzes, grades, and compressed handwriting files—is stored locally on your device. HomeSlate does not include built-in cloud sync; however, depending on your operating system settings, your device's standard OS backup features (local or cloud-based) may automatically include local app files, which is managed entirely by your operating system and outside the developer's control.

3. Children's Privacy

HomeSlate is built to protect children's privacy by keeping all data offline on your family's device. The app collects no personal information from children or adults, contains zero advertisements and zero third-party tracking or analytics SDKs, and requires a Parental Gate challenge before opening external links or making store purchases.

4. Data Control & Deletion

Parents have full control over all data stored in the app. You can edit or delete any child profile, quiz, or submission directly inside the app at any time, or remove all local data by uninstalling the app.

4. Website Hosting Notice & Deletion

This privacy policy is hosted via GitHub Pages. When you visit this page, GitHub may log your IP address as part of its standard security practices. Simply viewing this webpage does not transmit any family records or personal data from your app.
